Q.1 Multiple Choice Questions 2 × 5 = 10

1. Factor of (x−1) − (x²−1)

Options: (a) x²−1  (b) x+1  (c) x+1  (d) x+4
  • 1Pehle x²−1 ko factor karo — yeh ek identity hai: a²−b² = (a−b)(a+b). Toh x²−1 = (x−1)(x+1).
  • 2Ab expression bano: (x−1) − (x−1)(x+1)
  • 3Dono terms me common factor (x−1) hai — usko bahar nikaalo:
    (x−1)[1 − (x+1)] = (x−1)(1−x−1) = (x−1)(−x)
  • 4Toh poora expression = −x(x−1). Iske do factors hain: x aur (x−1).
🚩 Tere options me (b) aur (c) dono "x+1" print hue hain — yeh photo/OCR ka gadbad lagta hai, ek option asal me shaayad "x − 1" hoga. Sahi answer x − 1 hai — apne paper me jo option "x−1" ho, wahi tick karna (a) aur (d) galat hain kyunki wo factor hi nahi hain.

2. Sum jispe 2 saal, 5% CI se, byaj ₹164 aata hai

(a) ₹1600 (b) ₹1500 (c) ₹1400 (d) ₹1700
  • 1Compound Interest ka formula: A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ, aur CI = A − P
  • 2Yahan r=5%, n=2. Toh A = P(1.05)²= P × 1.1025
  • 3CI = P(1.1025 − 1) = 0.1025 P = 164
  • 4Toh P = 164 ÷ 0.1025 = 1600
✓ (a) ₹1600

3. Irrational number kaunsa hai

(a) √2a (b) √441 (c) 0.5948 (d) 5.3̄1̄8̄
  • 1Rule yaad rakh: koi bhi number jo terminate ho jaaye ya repeat (bar) kare — wo rational hota hai. Jo kabhi terminate/repeat nahi karta — irrational.
  • 2√441 = 21 → poora square number, so ek clean whole number = rational.
  • 30.5948 → yahin pe khatam ho gaya (terminating decimal) = rational.
  • 45.3̄1̄8̄ → bar hai matlab repeat ho raha hai = rational (koi bhi repeating decimal p/q me likha ja sakta hai).
  • 5√2 (ya √2 se bana koi surd jo perfect square na ho) kabhi terminate/repeat nahi karta = irrational.
✓ (a) √2a — kyunki baaki teeno rational nikal gaye

4. (2 − 3x)(5 − 2x) me x ka coefficient

(a) 19 (b) −19 (c) 15 (d) 6
  • 1Dono brackets ko multiply karo (har term ko har term se):
  • 22×5=10,   2×(−2x)=−4x,   (−3x)×5=−15x,   (−3x)×(−2x)=6x²
  • 3Sab jodo: 10 − 4x − 15x + 6x² = 6x² − 19x + 10
  • 4x ka coefficient (x ke saamne ka number) = −19
✓ (b) −19

5. (x − 1/x)(x + 1/x)(x² + 1/x²) ka product

(a) x⁴+1/x⁴ (b) x³+1/x³−2 (c) x⁴−1/x⁴ (d) x²+1/x²+2
  • 1Pehle do brackets uthao — yeh (a−b)(a+b)=a²−b² pattern hai:
    (x−1/x)(x+1/x) = x² − 1/x²
  • 2Ab isko third bracket se multiply karo — phir se a²−b² pattern:
    (x²−1/x²)(x²+1/x²) = x⁴ − 1/x⁴
✓ (c) x⁴ − 1/x⁴

Q.2 Solve all parts 3 × 5 = 15

(a) ½ aur ¾ ke beech ek rational number dhoondo

  • 1Do numbers ke exactly beech me jo number hota hai wo unka average (mean) hota hai: (a+b)/2
  • 2Pehle dono ka common denominator banao: ½ = 2/4, aur ¾ already hai.
  • 3(2/4 + 3/4)/2 = (5/4)/2 = 5/8
  • 4Check kar le: 5/8 = 0.625, jo 0.5 aur 0.75 ke beech me hi hai ✓
✔ Answer: 5/8

(b) Express 6.4̄6̄ (repeating) ko p/q form me likho

🚩 Photo me yeh number thoda unclear print hua hai. Maine assume kiya ki number hai 6.4646464... (yaani "46" baar-baar repeat ho raha hai) — kyunki isse answer bahut clean nikalta hai. Apne paper me overline (bar) exactly kis digit ke upar hai, wo ek baar check kar lena.
  • 1Repeating decimal ko p/q me convert karne ka trick: pehle usko x maan lo.
    Let x = 6.464646...
  • 2Jitne digits repeat ho rahe hain (yahan 2 digits: "46"), utni jagah decimal ko right shift karo — matlab 100 se multiply karo:
    100x = 646.464646...
  • 3Ab dono ko subtract karo — repeating part cancel ho jaayega:
    100x − x = 646.4646... − 6.4646...
    99x = 640
  • 4x = 640/99
✔ Answer: 640⁄99

(c) ₹4913, 1½ saal me, 12½% CI half-yearly compounded — sum kya thi?

  • 1Half-yearly matlab interest saal me 2 baar lagta hai. Toh rate aadha ho jaata hai aur time double:
    Half-yearly rate = 12.5% ÷ 2 = 6.25%, aur time = 1.5 × 2 = 3 half-years.
  • 26.25% ko fraction me likho: 6.25/100 = 1/16
  • 3Formula: A = P(1 + 1/16)³ = P × (17/16)³
  • 417³ = 4913 aur 16³ = 4096 (ye yaad rakhne wali cheez nahi, calculate kar sakte ho: 17×17=289, 289×17=4913)
  • 5Toh 4913 = P × 4913/4096 → dono taraf 4913 se cancel hota hai:
    P = 4096
✔ Answer: ₹4096
💡 Notice kiya? 17³ exactly 4913 nikla — paper banane wale ne jaan-boojh kar numbers aise choose kiye taaki cancel ho jaaye. Exam me aise "clean number" dikhe toh samajh jaana, tareeka sahi chal raha hai.

(d) Agar (x + 1/x) = 3, toh (x² + 1/x²) aur (x⁴ + 1/x⁴) nikaalo

  • 1Identity yaad rakho: (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
  • 2Yahan a=x, b=1/x. Toh (x+1/x)² = x² + 2·x·(1/x) + 1/x² = x² + 2 + 1/x²
  • 3Given hai (x+1/x)=3, toh dono side square karo: 3² = x² + 2 + 1/x² → 9 = x²+1/x² + 2
  • 4x² + 1/x² = 9 − 2 = 7
  • 5Ab x⁴+1/x⁴ ke liye same trick dobara: (x²+1/x²)² = x⁴ + 2 + 1/x⁴
  • 67² = x⁴+1/x⁴ + 2 → 49 = x⁴+1/x⁴ + 2
  • 7x⁴ + 1/x⁴ = 49 − 2 = 47
✔ Answer: x²+1/x² = 7, aur x⁴+1/x⁴ = 47

(e) Factorise: −25 + b²/64

  • 1Pehle order thoda seedha karo: b²/64 − 25
  • 2Dono terms ko "square" ki form me socho:
    b²/64 = (b/8)²  aur  25 = 5²
  • 3Ab yeh a² − b² wala pattern ban gaya (identity: a²−b² = (a−b)(a+b)), jahan a = b/8, b = 5
✔ Answer: (b/8 − 5)(b/8 + 5)

Q.3 Solve any five parts 4 × 5 = 20

(a) Factorise: 49x⁴ + 168x²y² + 144y⁴

  • 1Jab bhi teen terms ho aur pehla-aakhri dono perfect squares lage, perfect square trinomial check karo: a² + 2ab + b² = (a+b)²
  • 249x⁴ = (7x²)², aur 144y⁴ = (12y²)² — dono perfect squares hain ✓
  • 3Ab middle term check karo: agar a=7x², b=12y², toh 2ab = 2 × 7x² × 12y² = 168x²y² — exactly match ho gaya! ✓
✔ Answer: (7x² + 12y²)²

(b) (3+√2)/(3−√2) ko a + b√2 form me likho, a aur b nikaalo

✍️ Teri copy me answer "11.6√2" likha hai — yeh galat hai. Neeche sahi tareeka dekh:
  • 1Jab denominator me surd ho (√2), usko hataane ke liye rationalize karte hain — numerator aur denominator dono ko denominator ke conjugate se multiply karo. Conjugate of (3−√2) hai (3+√2).
  • 2(3+√2)/(3−√2) × (3+√2)/(3+√2)
  • 3Denominator: (3−√2)(3+√2) = 3² − (√2)² = 9 − 2 = 7 (a²−b² identity)
  • 4Numerator: (3+√2)(3+√2) = (3+√2)²= 3² + 2×3×√2 + (√2)² = 9 + 6√2 + 2 = 11 + 6√2
  • 5Toh poora expression = (11+6√2)/7 = 11/7 + (6/7)√2
✔ Answer: a = 11/7,   b = 6/7
💡 Yaad rakh: a+b√2 form me a aur b hamesha rational numbers hote hain (fractions chalenge), decimal "11.6" jaisa kuch nahi hota — isliye jab bhi surd-wala answer decimal me aaye, samajh jaana kahin calculation me mistake hai.

(c) ₹5,00,000 ka flat, 10% p.a. depreciate ho raha — kितने saal me value ₹3,64,500 rah jaayegi?

✍️ Teri copy me "6 years" likha hai — yeh bhi galat hai. Sahi answer 3 saal hai, dekh kaise:
  • 1Depreciation matlab value ghat rahi hai, toh formula me minus lagega:
    V = P(1 − r/100)ⁿ
  • 2Values daalo: 3,64,500 = 5,00,000 × (1 − 10/100)ⁿ = 5,00,000 × (0.9)ⁿ
  • 3Dono side 5,00,000 se divide karo: 3,64,500 ÷ 5,00,000 = 0.729 = (0.9)ⁿ
  • 4Ab (0.9) ko khud se multiply karte jao jab tak 0.729 na mile:
    0.9¹=0.9,   0.9²=0.81,   0.9³=0.729 ← match!
✔ Answer: n = 3 saal

(d) a² + b² + c² ka value, agar a+b+c=17 aur ab+bc+ca = ?

🚩 Photo me is sawaal ka doosra data (ab+bc+ca ki value) print/crop nahi hua — sirf "ab+bc+ca" likha dikh raha hai, koi number uske saath nahi. Please apne paper se wo missing number check karke bata dena, main turant final answer nikaal dunga. Tab tak, poora method samjha deta hoon taaki tu khud number aate hi solve kar sake:
  • 1Identity yaad rakho: (a+b+c)² = a²+b²+c² + 2(ab+bc+ca)
  • 2Isko rearrange kar do: a²+b²+c² = (a+b+c)² − 2(ab+bc+ca)
  • 3a+b+c=17 daalo: (17)² = 289
  • 4Jaise hi tujhe ab+bc+ca ki value mile, use 2 se multiply karke 289 me se ghata dena — final answer aa jaayega.
    Example (agar maan lo ab+bc+ca = 116 hota): 289 − 2(116) = 289−232 = 57.
✔ Formula ready hai: a²+b²+c² = 289 − 2(ab+bc+ca) — number bhejte hi answer final ho jaayega

(f) District me 64000 log, population 2½% p.a. badh rahi — 3 saal baad kitne honge?

  • 1Population badh rahi hai toh plus wala formula: A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ
  • 2P=64000, r=2.5%=140, n=3. Toh A = 64000 × (41/40)³
  • 341³ = 41×41×41 = 68921, aur 40³ = 64000
  • 4A = 64000 × 68921/64000 — 64000 cancel ho jaata hai dono taraf se!
✔ Answer: 68,921 inhabitants
💡 Fir se dekh — numbers itni cleanly cancel hui kyunki 2.5% = 1/40 hota hai, aur 41³/40³ exact whole number aata hai. Jab bhi percentage "clean fraction" ban rahi ho (jaise 2.5%, 5%, 12.5%, 25%), turant socho konsa cube/square yaad hai.

Q.4 Solve any one part (exam me sirf ek karna hai, yahan dono samjha raha hoon) 1 × 5 = 5

(a) Simplify: 6√2/(√3+√6) − 4√3/(√6+√2) + 2√6/(√2+√3)

  • 1Teeno terms ko alag-alag rationalize karenge — har denominator ke conjugate se multiply karke.
  • 2Term 1: 6√2/(√3+√6) ko (√6−√3) se multiply karo:
    Denominator: (√6)²−(√3)² = 6−3 = 3
    Numerator: 6√2(√6−√3) = 6√12 − 6√6 = 12√3 − 6√6 (kyunki √12=2√3)
    Term 1 = (12√3−6√6)/3 = 4√3 − 2√6
  • 3Term 2: 4√3/(√6+√2) ko (√6−√2) se multiply karo:
    Denominator: 6−2=4
    Numerator: 4√3(√6−√2) = 4√18 − 4√6 = 12√2 − 4√6 (kyunki √18=3√2)
    Term 2 = (12√2−4√6)/4 = 3√2 − √6
  • 4Term 3: 2√6/(√2+√3) ko (√3−√2) se multiply karo:
    Denominator: 3−2=1
    Numerator: 2√6(√3−√2) = 2√18 − 2√12 = 6√2 − 4√3
    Term 3 = 6√2 − 4√3
  • 5Ab jodo: Term1 − Term2 + Term3
    = (4√3−2√6) − (3√2−√6) + (6√2−4√3)
  • 6√3 wale: 4√3 − 4√3 = 0
    √6 wale: −2√6 + √6 = −√6
    √2 wale: −3√2 + 6√2 = 3√2
✔ Answer: 3√2 − √6

(b) x³ + 1/(125x³) nikaalo, agar x² + 1/(25x²) = 8⅗

  • 1Pehle 8⅗ ko improper fraction banao: 8⅗ = 43/5
  • 2Trick: maan lo a = x aur b = 1/(5x). Dekho kyun — b² = 1/(25x²), jo exactly given expression me hai! Aur b³ = 1/(125x³), jo exactly wahi hai jo humein chahiye.
  • 3Toh given: a² + b² = 43/5
  • 4Aur ab = x × 1/(5x) = 1/5 (x cancel ho gaya)
  • 5Ab identity use karo: (a+b)² = a²+2ab+b²
    (a+b)² = 43/5 + 2×(1/5) = 43/5 + 2/5 = 45/5 = 9
  • 6Toh a+b = √9 = 3
  • 7Ab humein a³+b³ chahiye. Identity: a³+b³ = (a+b)(a²−ab+b²)
  • 8a²−ab+b² = (a²+b²) − ab = 43/5 − 1/5 = 42/5
  • 9a³+b³ = 3 × 42/5 = 126/5
✔ Answer: 126/5 (= 25.2)
💡 Yeh question dikhne me daunting lagta hai, lekin trick yeh hai: jaise hi dekho "x² + 1/(kx²)" wala pattern given hai aur "x³ + 1/(mx³)" wala poochha ja raha hai, turant a=x, b=1/(√m · x) jaisa kuch treat karke a²+b² se shuru karo. Yeh ek bahut common exam trick hai.
